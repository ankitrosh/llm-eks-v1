## Known Limitations

- Aggregate throughput is not per-request throughput.
- No matched V3 single-request performance baseline.
- No full-model numerical comparison against an
  independent, unsplit reference implementation.
- No sequential-versus-concurrent token equivalence test.
- No production-scale sustained load or fault-injection test.
- The contribution of individual V2 optimizations to this
  benchmark has not been isolated.
- BF16 eager-attention performance is not directly equivalent
  to prior GPTQ/Marlin or SDPA results.

## Freeze Decision

Freeze V3 architecture and benchmark implementations.

Future optimizations and additional validation should be
tracked separately. Preserve historical benchmark logs,
regression tests, and reproducible deployment manifests.
